## Description

This is a hand-coloured manuscript plan drawn directly on the 1911 "Map of Contour Survey of the Site for the Federal Capital", whose title cartouche, notes box, and red and brown contour lines are clearly visible. The plan dams the Molonglo at the west ("DAM", "SPILLWAY") to form one large irregular lake. The lake follows the flood contour, has islands, and runs east to a second island near the "Baths". Formal centres on the south side sit around Camp Hill and Kurrajong ("Park") and a circus near the station. The north side has a large circus with radiating avenues and the "Aeroplane and Military Parade Reserve". At full resolution, the streets, lot subdivisions, numbered public buildings and red sewer and rising-main lines are all clearly legible.
