## Description

This is a monochrome 1913 Department of Lands lithograph of the plan, overprinted on the standard 1911 contour survey base map. The base map's cartouche, notes box and contours are visible. Upstream, in the west, the Molonglo keeps its natural meanders. Through the city centre it is straightened into a formal, rectangular-edged channel with long basins. The plan is dominated by a huge ring boulevard enclosing the northern city and a second, south-western arc, crossed by radial avenues and a long straight railway and avenue corridor to the south-east. A government group sits around Kurrajong with formal buildings south of the river. At full resolution, the dense street grid, blocks and tree-lined avenues are cleanly legible.
