## Description

This is a monochrome 1913 Department of Lands lithograph of Agache's plan, overprinted on the standard 1911 contour survey base map. The base map's cartouche, notes box and contours are fully visible, and an added title reads "Schematic Plan of the Town with General Study of Sewerage". Sewer collector lines, a legend and an inset of collector sections are also overprinted. The Molonglo is left largely as its natural meandering course, labelled "Molonglo River", with no formal lake. The town is organised as large wedge and fan-shaped sectors of long straight avenues. Monumental building groups sit on Camp Hill ("Campbell"), on a ring around Kurrajong, and in a large formal group to the south-east. At full resolution, the streets and building footprints are readable but crowded by the contours and sewer lines.
