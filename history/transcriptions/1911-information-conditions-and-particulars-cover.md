# Information, Conditions and Particulars (1911) — cover

> **About this transcription.**
> This is the cover of the booklet issued to entrants in the 1911 federal capital design competition.
> It was transcribed by hand from the National Library of Australia's scan (`nla.obj-232173787`, downloaded as `nla.obj-232173787 (1).zip`).
> The NLA has digitised only this one page.
> Its copy belongs to an incomplete set catalogued as *Competition design of Federal Capital City, Commonwealth of Australia: [information for competitors]* (`nla.obj-232173765`).
> The conditions themselves are on the pages inside, which this scan does not include.
> The 1912 Board report quotes the conditions' own wording, "information, conditions, and particulars issued for guidance in the preparation of these Designs", in the Minister's decision; see [1912-federal-capital-designs-board-report.md](1912-federal-capital-designs-board-report.md).

**FEDERAL CAPITAL CITY**

C.5381.
