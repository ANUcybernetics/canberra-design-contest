# Map of Contour Survey of the Site for the Federal Capital of Australia (1910) — Sheet No. 1

> **About this transcription.**
> This is a map, not a text document, so what follows is a transcription of the printed words on it, with a short description.
> It was read from the National Library of Australia's scan (`nla.obj-232175459`, downloaded as `nla.obj-232175459.zip`).
> The full-resolution TIFF in this folder is the same sheet and was not used.
> This map was issued to competitors with the conditions, and most of the plans reproduced in the 1912 Board report are drawn over it; see [1912-federal-capital-designs-board-report.md](1912-federal-capital-designs-board-report.md).
> Spot heights and contour values (in feet) are too numerous to transcribe and are left out.

## What the sheet shows

The sheet covers the north-western part of the city site, centred on the Molonglo River.
Contours are printed in red at 5-foot intervals, with heavier index contours, from about 1,780 feet at the river to just over 2,000 feet on the slopes of Black Mountain.
Watercourses and the flood limit are in blue, and the river bed is shaded grey.
Timbered country is outlined with a scalloped tree line.

The river enters at the bottom right, loops west in two broad meanders, and leaves at the left edge.
A label beside the title marks **Black Mountain** (2006).
The homestead **Sullivan** is marked at the foot of the steep slopes in the north-west.
A **Proposed Railway** runs north–south across the eastern part of the sheet, with bench marks of 1869·18 beside it.
